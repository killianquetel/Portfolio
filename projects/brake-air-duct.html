Dassault Systèmes · F1 virtual twin · 2025

Brake air duct entry

Front-corner brake cooling for the 2025 car: the inlet scoop, drum fairing and outlet vanes, all packaged inside the wheel.

Role
Design & surfacing
Tools
CATIA GSD+, Live Rendering
Year
2025

What I did

  • Inlet shaped to capture cooling air for the brakes while staying inside the wheel envelope.
  • Drum cover with slots and winglets that control where the hot air and the wheel wake go.
  • Modeled with GSD+ surfaces in the context of the wheel, tyre and upright.
