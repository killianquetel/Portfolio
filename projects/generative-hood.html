Dassault Systèmes · Modular design

Generative hood

A modular hood structure that moves from one car to another: the same modules rebuild on a supercar and on an e-car styling.

Tools
CATIA GSD+, modules
→

How it works

  • Inputs normalized and oriented by a positioning axis, so the structure depends on a few clean references.
  • Hinge, anti-flexion, latch reinforcement and inner structure as modules with published interfaces.
  • Structural alternatives switched without remodeling; one structure reused on three vehicles.
