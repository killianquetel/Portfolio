Dassault Systèmes · 3DEXPERIENCE R2024x

Parametrized SmallSat with reflectarray antenna

A parametric small-satellite model that adapts to standard and non-standard CubeSat sizes, built to show how the 3DEXPERIENCE platform can carry a space project from the first idea to launch.

Apps
Part Design, GSD, Assembly Design, Parametric Design Study
Year
2024
  1. 1Reference geometryESA M-Argo and NASA MarCO 12U CubeSats as starting point.
  2. 2Project structureParameters, model structure and alternatives defined before any geometry.
  3. 3ReflectarrayCircular pattern of square patches, fully parametric.
  4. 4Main structureDimensions driven by parameters, so every component follows.
  5. 5Solar panelsDeployable wings, animated from stowed to fully deployed.
  6. 6Design space checksParameters swept to prove the model rebuilds in every configuration.

Why it matters

  • Why reflectarray: a flat panel of patches recreates the field of a curved reflector, so the antenna folds flat against the satellite for launch.
  • One model, many missions: a standard platform that scales in size and performance, for a single satellite or a constellation.
  • Builds on my DCUBED work on deployable reflectarray antennas (ESA ARTES, IAC 2024).
